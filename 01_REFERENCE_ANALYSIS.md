## 1. Full transcripts

### V1: VW Arteon
> If you want the sleek looks of an Audi RS7 without paying Audi money, this is one of the most beautiful hidden gems on the used market. At first glance, it just looks like a massive, quiet luxury car for wealthy executives. I am talking about the Volkswagen Arteon. Because it wears a VW badge instead of four rings, most people completely ignore it. Everyone assumes it is just a slow, overgrown Passat built to blend into highway traffic. But what they don't realize is that hiding underneath this custom-tailored four-door coupe body is the exact same powertrain as the legendary Golf R. It packs the highly proven EA888 2.0-liter turbocharged engine paired with a dual clutch transmission, and it is an absolute tuning cheat code. The mod path to wake this luxury yacht up is dangerously simple. Throw on a high-flow downpipe, drop in an upgraded front-mount intercooler, and flash a custom Stage 2 engine and transmission tune. That is it. Bolt-ons and software. Suddenly, this quiet commuter jumps to nearly 400 wheel horsepower. Thanks to the 4Motion all-wheel drive system, it grips like absolutely crazy from a dig. The transmission snaps through gears flawlessly, it pulls relentlessly on the highway, and it will effortlessly gap loud Scat Packs, modern 5.0 Mustangs, and cocky BMW owners who had no idea what they were lining up against. It is basically a luxury Golf R wearing an undercover Audi business suit. Would you daily this all-wheel drive street assassin, or are you just going to buy an Audi like everybody else?

### V2: Cadillac CTS-V
> If you want a 900 horsepower supercar killer in luxury, this is one of the most insane builds you can do, and it will absolutely destroy anything on the street. The foundation is the 2016 to 2019 Cadillac CTS-V. Packing the same 6.2-liter supercharged LT4 V8 you'd find in a Corvette Z06 or Camaro ZL1. You can grab one for around 55 grand, and that's wild considering you're getting a hand-built monster straight from GM's performance division, wrapped in a luxury shell with heated seats and massagers. Now here's where things get crazy. Throw on a smaller supercharger pulley to spin that blower faster, a high-flow intake to let it breathe, and finish it off with a proper tune. That combo alone bumps power to around 750 horsepower on pump gas, but if you feed it E85 and add a set of long tube headers, you're looking at 900 horsepower on stock internals. It's quiet when you want it to be, violent when you floor it, and it'll embarrass supercars while looking like a CEO's daily driver. So tell me, are you keeping it mild at 750 horsepower or turning it up to 900?

### V3: Cadillac CTS V-Sport
> Everyone talks about the CTS-V, but the real hidden gem is this lighter, cheaper, and way more tunable beast. The foundation is the 2014 to 2019 Cadillac CTS V-Sport. Since everyone's obsessed with the supercharged CTS-V, these twin-turbo V6 models have dropped in value big time. You can pick one up clean for under 20 grand, and under the hood sits a 3.6-liter twin-turbo V6 pushing 420 horsepower stock. Now here's where it gets fun. Add a performance intake to let those turbos breathe. Upgrade the downpipes to help the exhaust flow. Drop in a larger intercooler to keep temps down, and finish it all off with a proper tune. The result? Over 500 horsepower, and torque numbers that rival the more expensive CTS-V, all while being lighter, cheaper, and way more reliable to daily. This thing pulls, sounds mean with downpipes, and flies under the radar since everyone assumes it's just a slow Cadillac. So would you build this sleeper?

## 2. The script formula (all 3 follow it)

| Beat | Time | What's said | Example |
|------|------|-------------|---------|
| **1. Hook** | 0–5 s | Big promise, compared against a famous expensive car or a big HP number | "...the sleek looks of an Audi RS7 without paying Audi money" |
| **2. Reveal** | 5–12 s | "The foundation is the **[YEARS] [MAKE MODEL]**" | "The foundation is the 2016 to 2019 Cadillac CTS-V" |
| **3. Underdog angle** | 12–22 s | Why people ignore it or why it's cheap. The "sleeper" story | "Everyone assumes it is just a slow, overgrown Passat" |
| **4. Engine flex** | 22–32 s | Engine code, displacement, induction, shared with a famous car | "Same 6.2 supercharged LT4 you'd find in a Corvette Z06" |
| **5. Price** | ~25–35 s | "You can grab one for around X grand" | "under 20 grand" |
| **6. Transition line** | — | "Now here's where things get crazy / fun" | |
| **7. Mod list** | 35–50 s | 3–4 mods, each with a short "why" (verb + part + reason) | "a smaller pulley to spin that blower faster" |
| **8. Result** | 50–60 s | Final HP number, "on pump gas", "on stock internals" | "nearly 400 wheel horsepower" |
| **9. Driving and victims** | 55–70 s | How it feels, plus which famous cars it embarrasses | "gap Scat Packs, 5.0 Mustangs and cocky BMW owners" |
| **10. One-liner metaphor** | | Memorable summary | "a luxury Golf R wearing an undercover Audi business suit" |
| **11. CTA question** | last 4 s | Either/or question to drive comments | "Keeping it mild at 750 or turning it up to 900?" |

**Writing rules observed**
- 160–210 words, read at about 2.6–2.8 words per second, fast and confident. No pauses.
- Second person ("you can grab one", "throw on").
- Power verbs: *throw on, drop in, flash, bump, wake up, gap, embarrass, destroy*.
- Repeated sleeper vocabulary: *hidden gem, sleeper, flies under the radar, cheat code, undercover*.
- Numbers are always rounded and hedged: "around", "nearly", "over".
- Every mod comes with a reason: "to let it breathe", "to keep temps down".

## 3. Visual / editing style

**Canvas**
- 1080×1920 export (refs are 576×1024). Flat light-grey background **#D5D5D5**. No gradients and no video footage.
- **Top half (y ≈ 5–45 %)** holds the car or parts zone. **Bottom half (y ≈ 50–100 %)** holds the character.

**Character (bottom)**
- A full-body cartoon mascot standing centred and facing the camera.
- Animation: about 6–8 pose/expression swaps (arms out, pointing up, shrug, open-mouth "talking", surprised face), switching about every 1–2 s, with lip-flap during speech.
- During the **mod list**, the character **shrinks to about 45 % and moves bottom-right**, which frees the lower-left area for part images.

**Car / parts (top)**
- AI-rendered "studio cutout" images (PNG, transparent background) of the exact car, usually customised: widebody, dark wheels, red calipers.
- A new car angle about every 2–4 s: front 3/4, rear 3/4, side profile, front-on.
- The brand logo appears large above the car at hook and CTA moments, and small next to the HP badge.
- Two-car diagonal layout (one small top-left, one large bottom-right) for the "underdog / everyone ignores it" beat.
- Engine beat: **engine-bay shot + isolated engine** side by side.
- Mod beat: a part image (downpipe, intercooler, pulley, intake, headers, tune chip icon) pops in for each mod as it's spoken.

**Text overlays**
| Type | Style | Example |
|------|-------|---------|
| Spoken caption | **One word at a time**, bold white, thick black outline, centred at y ≈ 47 % (between car and character) | `beautiful` |
| Title card | White bold text in a black rounded pill, top centre | `VOLKSWAGEN ARTEON` |
| Year + model | Large black bold year, small black model name under it, typewriter reveal | `2016-2019` / `CADILLAC CTS-V` |
| Engine spec | Black bold (V1/V2) or **blue #1E90FF** (V3), typewriter | `6.2L SUPERCHARGED` |
| Mod name | **Blue #1E90FF** bold caps | `DOWNPIPES`, `INTERCOOLER`, `TUNE` |
| Price | **Green #22C55E** with dark outline (car price) or blue (part price) | `$55,000`, `$400` |
| HP badge | **Blue #1E90FF** bold next to the small logo | `400HP`, `900HP` |

**Motion**
- Elements pop in with a quick scale-up (about 0.15 s). Text types on letter by letter.
- Hard cuts on every sentence. Nothing stays static for more than about 3 s.
- Small fades or ghosting when cars swap (see the faded car at V2 0:00).

**Audio**
- A single energetic male narrator voiceover, normalised loud (peaks at 0 dB). No background music under the voice. V2 adds a short music sting only on the outro.
- No sound effects.

**Outro**
- About 2 s TikTok end card. We will replace it with your own handle card.
