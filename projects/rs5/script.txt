If you want the performance of an Audi R8 without paying Audi R8 money, this is one of the most underrated German performance cars on the used market. The foundation is the 2018 to 2020 Audi RS5 B9. Since everyone's obsessed with expensive RS models and supercars, most people overlook just how much performance this thing offers for the money. You can find used examples for around 40 grand, and under the hood sits a 2.9-liter twin-turbocharged V6 producing 444 horsepower and 443 pound-feet of torque straight from the factory, and it comes with Audi's quattro all-wheel drive system, making it an absolute weapon from a standstill. Now, here's where things get crazy. Start with a high-flow performance intake to help the turbos breathe. Add an upgraded turbo inlet to improve airflow, and finish it all off with a proper ECU and transmission tune. The result? Around 510 to 520 horsepower on pump gas, depending on the setup and tuner. And the crazy part is that you can achieve those numbers without even changing the factory turbos. It launches hard, shifts incredibly fast, and grips like crazy thanks to quattro. It can embarrass much more expensive sports cars while still looking like an elegant German luxury coupe. It's basically a supercar performance package hiding inside an executive Audi. So, would you build this twin-turbo RS5, or spend the extra money on an actual R8?
