If you want the sleek looks of an Audi RS5 without paying RS5 money, this is one of the most beautiful hidden gems on the used market. At first glance, it just looks like an expensive German luxury coupe built for people who want to arrive in style. I'm talking about the 2008–2016 Audi A5 2.0T. Because it doesn't have an S or RS badge, most people completely underestimate it. They assume it's just another slow luxury car designed to cruise down the highway. But hiding underneath that elegant body is the highly tunable EA888 2.0-liter turbocharged four-cylinder. Depending on the year and market, it produces around 208 to 220 horsepower and 258 pound-feet of torque. And this engine has serious tuning potential. The mod path to wake this German coupe up is surprisingly simple. Start with a high-flow intake, upgrade the intercooler, install a performance downpipe, and finish everything with a custom Stage 2 ECU tune. That's when things get interesting. With the right supporting hardware and fuel, tuned examples can approach 280–290 horsepower, depending on the setup. And if you want to take things even further, a K04 turbo upgrade can push this platform into another performance category. Suddenly, this elegant Audi isn't just another luxury commuter. It becomes a sharp, turbocharged street sleeper with the styling of a much more expensive German performance car. Basically, it's an affordable RS-inspired coupe hiding serious potential underneath that clean Audi body. Would you build this A5 into a sleeper, or save your money for a proper RS model?
