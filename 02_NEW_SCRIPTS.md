# New Video Scripts (5 videos)

Every script follows the 11-beat formula from `01_REFERENCE_ANALYSIS.md`.
Target: 170–200 words, which is about 65–80 s with an energetic ElevenLabs read.

**Shot-list legend** (what goes in the top half while the line is spoken)
- `CAR:` an AI car cutout (angle given) · `LOGO` brand logo · `PILL:` black title pill
- `YEAR:` black year + model typewriter · `SPEC:` engine text · `PART:` part image + blue label (+ price)
- `HP:` blue HP badge · `$:` green price · `CHAR:` character pose / position

> Figures are typical, published, community-reported numbers, rounded and hedged ("around", "nearly") the same way the references do. Used prices are approximate US market values and drift over time.
