Sound effects used in the car videos

1_car_appear.wav            - when a car shot appears (taken from the reference videos). Starts 15 ms before the shot.
2_shine.wav                 - the shine flash on the car (taken from the reference videos). Starts on the first flash frame.
3_part_appear.wav           - when a part / number / label appears (custom tonal pop + tick).
4_typing_click_single.wav   - one key click, played for every letter of the typed titles.
5_typing_sequence.wav       - example: a full typed title (22 letters per second).

Levels used in the mix: car appear = 1.14 x speech loudness, shine = 0.89 x, part = 0.85 x, typing clicks are quiet (0.12).
