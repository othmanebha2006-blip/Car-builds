Sound effects used in the car videos

1_car_appear.wav       - when a car shot appears (taken from the reference videos). Starts 15 ms before the shot.
2_shine.wav            - the shine flash on the car (taken from the reference videos). Starts on the first flash frame.
3_part_popup.wav       - when a part / upgrade / number pops in (extracted from your clip: rising pop 630->1290 Hz, 47 ms). On the frame it appears.
4_typing_reference.wav - the typing sound from the reference videos (a real keyboard typing recording, sped up x1.25, 1.7 s).
                         Start it with the first typed letter (28 ms before), cut it with a 30 ms fade when the typing stops.

Levels used in the mix (RMS vs speech): car appear = 1.14 x, shine = 0.89 x, part pop = 1.18 x, typing = 0.48 x.
