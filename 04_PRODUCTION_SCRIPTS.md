# Production Scripts (based on the viral reference videos)

These are the 3 scripts we produce first, using the user's versions of the reference scripts.
`[ENGINE SOUND]` = about 10 s of engine/rev audio with no voice. The car shots keep cutting during it.
The engine sound will be generated with ElevenLabs Sound Effects.

## Video 1: Cadillac CTS-V (about 78 s)

**Hook**
If you want a 900 horsepower supercar killer in luxury, this is one of the most insane builds you can do, and it will absolutely destroy anything on the street.

`[ENGINE SOUND: supercharged V8 revving, supercharger whine, ~10 s]`

**Foundation**
The foundation is the 2016 to 2019 Cadillac CTS-V, packing the same 6.2 liter supercharged LT4 V8 you'd find in a Corvette Z06 or Camaro ZL1. You can grab one for around 55 grand, and that's wild considering you're getting a hand-built monster straight from GM's performance division, wrapped in a luxury shell with heated seats and massagers.

**Mods**
Now, here's where things get crazy. Throw on a smaller supercharger pulley, a cooling system that keeps temps lower, a high-flow intake to let it breathe, and finish it off with a proper tune. That combo alone bumps power to around 750 horsepower on pump gas. But if you feed it E85 and add a set of long-tube headers, you're looking at 900 horsepower on stock internals.

**Outro / CTA**
It's quiet when you want it to be, violent when you floor it, and it'll embarrass supercars while looking like a CEO's daily driver. So tell me, are you keeping it mild at 750 horsepower, or turning it up to 900?

It pulls relentlessly on the highway, and it will effortlessly walk loud Scat Packs, modern 5.0 Mustangs, and cocky BMW owners who had no idea what they were lining up against. It's basically a luxury Golf R wearing an undercover Audi business suit.

**CTA**
